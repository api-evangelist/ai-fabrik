# AI Fabrik

> AI Fabrik is an edge inference delivery network — a "modular token factory" that
> deconstructs the traditional data center model and rebuilds every layer for real-time
> AI, delivering distributed inference close to users and cloud with sub-20ms latency to
> tier 1 and tier 2 markets. Incubated inside Gruve and led by founder Tarun Raisoni with
> Swati Deshpande and Tanuj Mohan, the company is deploying five initial production sites
> with 6,000+ B300 GPUs in 2026, backed by Mayfield, Xora (Temasek), Acclimate Ventures
> and Cisco Investments.

> Status for agents: AI Fabrik publishes NO public API, NO developer portal, NO
> machine-readable specification, and NO pricing. As of 2026-08-10 the entire public
> surface is a ten-page marketing site plus a blog. The commercial and technical entry
> point is a contact form with a stated 24-hour response window. This file is generated
> by API Evangelist from the public site; it is not published by AI Fabrik.
